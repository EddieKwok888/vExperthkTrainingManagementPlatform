# Training Team Handover Package

## Welcome to the ProTrain AI Management Platform!

This system replaces legacy spreadsheets with a unified, cloud-based platform covering public enrollment, tutor management, and AI-assisted support.

### Included Documents

Please review the generated documentation folder `docs/` for your daily operations:

1. **ADMIN_SOP.md**: Your daily step-by-step workflow for managing courses and payments.
2. **USER_GUIDES.md**: Guides to share with your Tutors and Students.
3. **INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md**: What to do if the system encounters an error.
4. **SUPPORT_WORKFLOW.md**: How to handle student escalations.

### System Overview & Roles

- **Admins:** The Training Management Team. Full control over approvals and course creation.
- **Tutors:** Contracted educators. Focused purely on attending sessions and tracking hours.
- **Students:** The end-users. Focused on registration, payments, and downloading certificates.

### Known Limitations (MVP Phase 1)

- Payments are manually verified via screenshot uploads (No Stripe/Credit Card auto-gateway yet).
- Tutor payroll is tracked here but dispersed offline.
- Certificate generation relies on Admins executing the process.

### Support Contact Process

For critical P1/P2 issues, please escalate to the designated SLA technical support vendor with the accompanying `error_logs` viewable from your Admin Dashboard.
