# User Guides

## Admin Guide

**Login Steps:**

1. Navigate to the landing page and click **Login / Register**.
2. Sign in with the registered Admin Google Account.
3. You will be automatically redirected to the **Admin Dashboard** (`/admin`).

**Key Functions:**

- **Overview:** View high-level KPIs and charts.
- **Courses:** Manage public course offerings.
- **Registrations:** Verify payments and manage student enrollment.
- **Logs:** Review immutable audit logs of system actions.

**Common Errors:**

- _Access Denied_: Ensure your account role is properly set to `admin` in the database setup.

---

## Tutor Guide

**Login Steps:**

1. Go to the application and login using your assigned Google Account.
2. Access the **Tutor Portal** via the navigation menu.

**Key Functions:**

- **My Sessions:** View upcoming assigned teaching sessions.
- **Attendance:** Mark student attendance after a session.
- **Hours Submission:** Submit your teaching hours for payroll processing.

**Common Errors & FAQ:**

- _I cannot see my session:_ Ensure the Admin has assigned you to the session using your exact Account ID.
- _How do I get paid?_ Submit your teaching hours; once marked `approved` by an Admin, processing will begin offline.

---

## Student / Public User Guide

**Login Steps:**

1. Browse available courses publicly without logging in.
2. To register, click **Register**, and sign in via Google.

**Key Functions:**

- **Browse Courses:** View all active training courses.
- **AI Chatbot:** Ask questions about course specifics.
- **Registration & Payment:** Enroll in a session and upload your bank transfer/PayMe screenshot.
- **My Courses:** Track your registration and payment status from the student dashboard.

**FAQ:**

- _How do I know my payment is verified?_ Check the `My Courses` tab. The status will change from `pending_verification` to `verified`.
- _I uploaded the wrong proof!_ Contact admin support directly until the proof rejection allows re-upload.
