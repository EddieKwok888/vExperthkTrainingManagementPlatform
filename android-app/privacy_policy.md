## 隱私權政策 (繁體中文)

**最後更新日期：2024年**

感謝您使用 **vExpert AI Training Academy** (以下簡稱「本應用程式」)。本應用程式非常重視您的隱私權。本隱私權政策說明了當您使用本應用程式時，我們如何收集、使用、保護及處理您的個人資料。

### 1. 我們收集的資料
當您使用本應用程式時，我們可能會收集以下類型的資訊：
* **個人識別資訊：** 當您註冊帳號時，我們可能會收集您的電子郵件地址、姓名及相關登入資訊 (透過 Firebase Authentication)。
* **學習與課程資料：** 您在應用程式內報名的課程、出席紀錄、個人課程時間表等資訊 (儲存於 Firebase Firestore)。
* **裝置權限 (相機)：** 本應用程式包含條碼/QR Code 掃描功能 (透過 Google ML Kit)。為了使用此功能，我們需要存取您的裝置相機。這些影像資訊僅會在您的裝置上進行即時處理，我們**不會**將您的相機影像傳送至我們的伺服器或儲存下來。

### 2. 資料的使用方式
我們收集的資料將用於以下目的：
* 建立並管理您的專屬學習帳號。
* 提供課程報名、進度追蹤及排程功能。
* 處理並確認您的課程出席紀錄。
* 寄送重要的課程通知、平台更新或公告。
* 改善並優化應用程式的使用者體驗。

### 3. 第三方服務與資料分享
本應用程式使用以下第三方服務，這些服務可能會收集用來識別您身分的資訊：
* **Google Play Services**
* **Firebase Authentication** (用於帳號登入與驗證)
* **Cloud Firestore** (用於儲存應用程式的雲端資料)
* **Google ML Kit** (用於裝置端的條碼掃描)

除了上述提供核心功能的第三方服務外，我們**不會**將您的個人資料出售、交易或轉讓給其他外部團體。

### 4. 資料安全
我們致力於保護您的個人資料安全。我們使用 Firebase 提供的安全加密技術來儲存和傳輸您的資料。然而，請注意，網際網路上的傳輸方法或電子儲存方法並非 100% 安全，我們無法保證其絕對的安全性。

### 5. 您的權利 (資料刪除)
您有權隨時要求刪除您的帳號及相關個人資料。如果您希望刪除您的資料，請透過應用程式內的設定或聯絡我們的支援團隊來提交刪除要求。

### 6. 隱私權政策的修改
我們保留隨時修改本隱私權政策的權利。如有任何重大變更，我們會在應用程式內發布通知。您繼續使用本應用程式即表示您同意修改後的隱私權政策。

## Privacy Policy (English)

**Last updated: 2024**

Thank you for using **vExpert AI Training Academy** ("the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

### 1. Information We Collect
We may collect the following types of information:
* **Personal Information:** When you register for an account, we may collect your email address, name, and login credentials (via Firebase Authentication).
* **Course and Learning Data:** Information regarding your enrolled courses, attendance records, and personal course schedules (stored via Firebase Firestore).
* **Device Permissions (Camera):** The App includes barcode/QR code scanning functionality (via Google ML Kit). To use this feature, we require access to your device's camera. The image data is processed locally on your device in real-time. We **do not** transmit or store your camera images on our servers.

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information for the following purposes:
* To create and manage your personal learning account.
* To provide course enrollment, progress tracking, and scheduling features.
* To process and verify your course attendance.
* To send important course notifications, updates, and announcements.
* To improve and optimize the user experience of the App.

### 3. Third-Party Services
The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. The third-party service providers used by the App include:
* **Google Play Services**
* **Firebase Authentication** (for account login and verification)
* **Cloud Firestore** (for cloud database storage)
* **Google ML Kit** (for on-device barcode scanning)

We **do not** sell, trade, or otherwise transfer to outside parties your personally identifiable information, except for the trusted third parties mentioned above who assist us in operating our application.

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information. We use Firebase's secure encryption technologies to store and transmit your data. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

### 5. Your Rights (Data Deletion)
You have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time. If you wish to delete your data, please submit a request through the settings within the App or by contacting our support team.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
